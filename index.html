Daily plan · weeks 19 to 40

Garbh Sanskar Planner

Music, meals, movement, mind and learning for each day until the baby arrives. Open it every morning; the Today tab shows exactly what to do.

Change it if your OB updates the date.

This planner follows the garbh sanskar tradition (music, mantra, calm focused activity, a sattvic diet) and pairs it with current US prenatal guidance from ACOG, CDC and AAP. It is a daily routine, not medical advice: keep every OB appointment, take the prenatal vitamin as prescribed, and clear the exercise routine with your OB. Stop any activity and call the OB for bleeding, fluid leaking, regular contractions, severe headache, vision changes, chest pain, calf pain, or reduced baby movement.
